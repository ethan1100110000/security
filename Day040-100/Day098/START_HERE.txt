Day098 - CFG 3: indirect call

Goal
- Find the function-pointer or indirect-call candidate in Ghidra.
- Explain where the call target comes from and how input selects it.
- Recover one input that prints ACCESS GRANTED.
- Cross-check at least once with GDB or objdump.

Binary
- day98_indirect_lab
- Linux x86-64 ELF, PIE, stripped
- SHA-256 is recorded in this folder after build.

Suggested order
1. Find main from the entry path.
2. Recover the input format and length/range checks.
3. Find the indirect call instruction.
4. Trace the function pointer backward to its table.
5. Analyze only the selected callee and invert its verifier.

Do not brute-force the final input. Record evidence in day98_rev.md.
