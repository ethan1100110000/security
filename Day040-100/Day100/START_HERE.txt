Day100 - Anti-debug 2: timing / trap

Goal
- Find the timing measurement and threshold comparison.
- Find the SIGTRAP handler and intentional INT3 instruction.
- Predict normal execution and execution under GDB.
- Verify one timing anomaly and one trap anomaly.
- Record at least one bypass idea for each check.
- Recover one input that prints ACCESS GRANTED.

Binary
- day100_timing_trap_lab
- Linux x86-64 ELF, PIE, stripped

Suggested order
1. Find main from the entry path.
2. Follow the timing-anomaly string XREF.
3. Identify start/end measurements and the measured region.
4. Follow the trap-anomaly string XREF.
5. Identify handler registration, INT3, and the checked flag.
6. Plan GDB verification before setting breakpoints.
7. Analyze the token verifier only after both checks are understood.

Do not brute-force the token. Keep the two anti-debug checks separate.
