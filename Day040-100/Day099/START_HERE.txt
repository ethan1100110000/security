Day099 - Anti-debug 1: ptrace pattern

Goal
- Find the anti-debug check in Ghidra and explain its evidence.
- Recover the normal input-validation flow hidden behind the check.
- Record at least two bypass ideas, then verify one in GDB.
- Recover one input that prints ACCESS GRANTED.

Binary
- day99_ptrace_lab
- Linux x86-64 ELF, PIE, stripped

Suggested order
1. Find main from the entry path.
2. Identify the ptrace call and its arguments.
3. Follow the return-value branch.
4. Predict normal execution and execution under GDB.
5. Choose one temporary GDB bypass.
6. Analyze the token verifier only after reaching it.

Do not brute-force the token. Record static evidence and dynamic evidence.
